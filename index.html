Lull

Five quiet minutes, at your desk, to put the day down.

  1. Pinch the ember. Thoughts drift up around you.
  2. Carry each one to a jar on your real desk. Tomorrow, Let it rest, or Not mine to carry.
  3. Breathe with the lantern in your hands while your room gently goes dark.
  4. Tonight becomes a star. Seven nights make a constellation.
Hands only. No controllers needed. Best seated.
